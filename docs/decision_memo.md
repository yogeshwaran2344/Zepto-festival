# Zepto Festive Demand Plan — Decision Memo

## Headline
Five of eight high-volume festive SKUs require 2,117 additional top-up units to achieve 80% demand coverage, despite overall commitments (33,170 units) trailing planned demand (33,656 units) by only 486 units (-1.44%).

## Context
Category leads must finalize supplier orders for weeks 89–92 across 20 SKUs. Committed quantities of 33,170 units trail planned demand of 33,656 units by 486 units (-1.44% committed units vs planned units). Stock-out incidence was 22.50% in festive weeks (18 of 80 SKU-weeks) versus 6.01% in regular weeks (101 of 1,680 SKU-weeks), demonstrating raw sales understate festive demand.

## Insight
Deficits concentrate in Sweets & Mithai (-490 committed vs planned units, -4.76%) and Pooja & Decor (-326 units, -4.43%). Among eight Focus SKUs, five need top-ups: Ghee 1 L (cover probability 26.00%, top-up 777 units), Kaju Katli (28.99%, top-up 535 units), Incense Sticks (47.11%, top-up 429 units), Cola 2 L (66.23%, top-up 249 units), and Diya Set (73.99%, top-up 127 units). Cashew Nuts (83.93%), Potato Chips (82.22%), and Dates (93.99%) are Covered. Hold-back testing confirms projection accuracy within -3.37% to +2.12%. No SKU is marked Review under shape_flag (largest mean-median gap is Ghee at 52.67 units, 0.43 × sd), validating the normality assumption.

## Recommendation
Contract 2,117 top-up units across the five at-risk SKUs by Oct 24 (Owner: Procurement Lead). Reallocate dark-store storage for Sweets (+1,312 units) and Pooja (+556 units) by Oct 26 (Owner: Fulfillment Lead). Deploy daily replenishment monitoring for Focus SKUs by Oct 27 (Owner: Inventory Lead).

## Limitations
1. **Unobserved Lead-Time and Supplier Reliability:** While historical lead times are documented, supplier fulfillment rates under surge conditions are unobserved; whether suppliers can deliver the 2,117 top-up units within existing lead times is cause unknown.
2. **Stationary Festive Uplift Assumption:** A single category uplift factor is applied uniformly across weeks 89–92. The analysis does not capture intra-festive week-on-week demand progression (e.g., peak demand spike immediately preceding Diwali vs. post-festival tapering), as intra-festival temporal shape remains unmodeled.
3. **Absence of Marketing, Pricing, and Cross-Elasticity Data:** Historical sales figures omit promotion schedules, app banner impressions, and dynamic price adjustments. If discounts or competitor stock-outs alter consumer demand across substitute SKUs, actual demand may diverge from clean-week projections; cause unknown for promotional elasticity.
